This is a static website. I don't run analytics, advertising or tracking on it, and it sets no cookies of its own.

## Hosting

The site is served by [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when pages and files are requested, as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). I don't have access to those logs.

## Downloads

Teaching slides and worksheets are plain files. I can't see who downloads them or how often.

## Email

If you email me, I'll keep your message and address so I can reply, and I won't share them or add you to a mailing list.
